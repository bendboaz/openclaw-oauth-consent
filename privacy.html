Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

“My Local OpenClaw” (“the application”) is a personal, single-user automation tool operated by one individual (“the owner”) on hardware they control. This policy explains how it handles data obtained through Google APIs.

Who uses this application

Only the owner. The application is not offered to, advertised to, or usable by any other person. The owner authorizes the application against their own Google account(s).

What data the application accesses

When the owner grants access, the application may read and modify data in the owner’s own Google account through Google services that may include:

  • Gmail — reading, searching, drafting, and sending the owner’s mail
  • Google Calendar — reading and managing the owner’s events
  • Google Contacts (People API) — reading, editing, and deleting the owner’s contacts
  • Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Slides — reading and editing the owner’s files
  • Google Tasks, Forms, and Meet — reading and managing the owner’s items

Access is limited to what the owner directs the application to do.

How the data is used

Data is used solely to carry out automation tasks the owner requests, on the owner’s behalf. Processing happens in memory and in local files on the owner’s own computer. The application does not run on any shared or third-party server.

Storage and retention

OAuth tokens and a limited amount of working data (for example, cached message or contact details needed to complete a task the owner asked for) are stored locally on the owner’s computer. There is no external database and no cloud storage operated by the application. The owner can remove this data at any time by deleting the local files and revoking access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sharing

The application does not sell, share, or transmit Google user data to any third party. It uses no analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Data does not leave the owner’s machine except in direct calls to Google’s own APIs and, where the owner explicitly directs it, to a private messaging channel the owner controls.

Limited Use

The application’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
