My Local OpenClaw

This is the home page for a personal, self-hosted automation gateway run by a single individual (“the owner”) on their own computer. It uses OpenClaw and gogcli to let the owner automate their own Google account — for example triaging their own email, checking their own calendar, and tidying their own contact list — through a private chat interface.

It is not a public service. Nobody other than the owner can sign in to it or use it, and it is not distributed to anyone else.

Privacy

The Privacy Policy describes what Google account data this application accesses and how that data is handled.
